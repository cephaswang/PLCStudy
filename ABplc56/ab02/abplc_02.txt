Industrial automation was started from complete manual operations involving lots of humans to handle the measurement and control applications in the industries.

Then it shifted from manual control to pneumatic sensors and controllers using pressure as the medium for the sensors measurement and control applications.

Later on, the industries were introduced relay logics and analog electronics.

Finally, industrial automation evolved to programmable logic controllers (PLC), distributed control systems (DCS), smart transmitters, and industrial internet of things (IIOT).
