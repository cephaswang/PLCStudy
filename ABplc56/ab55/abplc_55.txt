PLC 3-Way Traffic Light Control System | Ladder Logic Timer Sequence


This project demonstrates a PLC ladder logic program for controlling a 3-way intersection traffic light system. Each direction (1, 2, and 3) is given a 20-second green phase in sequence to ensure safe vehicle flow. Direction 4 operates differently and remains stopped except when direction 2 is active. The system uses timer-based sequencing and interlocking logic to coordinate traffic movement efficiently, ensuring safe and structured control of all directions in an automated traffic system.
