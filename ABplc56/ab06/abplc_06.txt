PLC Training 6 - Introduction to Allen Bradley PLC 



In this video, we learn about the introduction to Allen Bradley PLC.

Allen Bradley is the brand-name of factory automation equipment, today owned by Rockwell Automation. The company based in the United States of America.

They manufacture control systems, sensors, industrial control equipment, control panels, and automation software.

Types of Allen Bradley PLCs are 

1. Micro and Nano Control Systems
2. Small Control Systems
3. Large Control Systems
